Five-day focus rhythm

An optional sample routine · adjust around your own timetable and rest
Small, repeatable steps beat last-minute cramming
Monday · ResetPick one focus topic

Write a tiny goal for the week and gather your class notes.

Tuesday · PracticeTry, check, retry

Spend 20 minutes on a few Maths or Computer questions.

Wednesday · RecallExplain it simply

Summarize one Science idea without looking at your notes.

Thursday · ConnectRead and discuss

Review an English passage or ask a classmate a question.

Friday · ReflectNotice your progress

Celebrate one win, note one question, then make time to rest.
